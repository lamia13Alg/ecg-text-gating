# Licence of the audit files

The files in this folder contain machine-generated report text and record identifiers
taken from **MIMIC-IV-ECG v1.0** (Gow et al., PhysioNet, https://doi.org/10.13026/4nqg-sb35),
together with our annotations, sampling weights and cardiologist verification.

MIMIC-IV-ECG is distributed under the **Open Data Commons Open Database License v1.0 (ODbL)**.
As a derived database, the contents of this folder are therefore also made available under the
ODbL v1.0: https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/

When using these files, please cite MIMIC-IV-ECG (Gow et al., 2023), PhysioNet
(Pollard et al., 2026, https://doi.org/10.1038/s44360-026-00096-z) and our paper.
